08 / COURSEWORK · AER1216

Fixed-wing UAV modelling & control

The fixed-wing side of the UAV coursework: aircraft and propeller performance analysis (range, endurance, hover), a wing modelled in CAD, and a full 6-DoF Simulink simulation flown by cascaded control loops: altitude, pitch, roll, heading, and velocity.

1.Airframe & performance

Before anything flies, the numbers: propeller performance curves, and range, endurance, and hover analysis for fixed-wing and multirotor configurations, worked in the performance report. The wing itself was modelled in CAD with control surfaces cut in.

2.Simulink flight control

The simulation is a full 6-DoF fixed-wing model in Simulink, flown by cascaded loops: an outer altitude-hold loop commanding pitch, an inner pitch controller driving elevator deflection, with roll, heading, and velocity/climb loops alongside and saturation limits between the controllers and the dynamics.

3.Flying the model

The closed-loop model flies missions in the MATLAB animation below: takeoff, climb, and cruise legs commanded by the mission state machine, with the aircraft tracking through the world frame.

4.Credits

Team coursework with Feiyang Wu and Clarence Zhang. The quadrotor half of this course lineage lives in project 04.
