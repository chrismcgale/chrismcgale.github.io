07 / FABRICATION BACKBONE

Voron 2.4

A CoreXY printer built entirely from parts (frame, belts, wiring, firmware), running Klipper with sensorless homing. Not a project for its own sake: it's the machine that fabricates parts for everything else on this site.

Status
Operational · daily driver
Kinematics
CoreXY, flying gantry (2.4)
Firmware
Klipper · sensorless homing
Build
Full from-parts, no kit shortcuts

1.Why build, not buy

A Voron 2.4 from parts is a complete mechatronics curriculum in one machine: frame squaring, belt tensioning, four-motor flying-gantry kinematics, wiring harness design, and a firmware stack (Klipper) that rewards actually understanding the hardware under it. Sensorless homing replaces physical endstops with stallguard current sensing on the steppers: fewer parts, cleaner motion, one more thing tuned rather than bolted on.

2.What it feeds

Every hardware project on this site has parts that came off this bed:

  • the ESP-DIVE submarine's 3D-printed hull,
  • enclosures for the kickbox strike pods,
  • mounts and structural parts for the drone builds, including the Rinnegan hardware target.

That's the honest framing: this machine is infrastructure. It converts CAD into iterations, and iteration speed is the real spec.
